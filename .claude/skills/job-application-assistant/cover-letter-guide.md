# Cover letters

Many Indian startups never read them, so ask before writing one. Write one when the application form has a cover-letter or "Why us?" field, when the posting asks for it, for MNC and GCC roles, or for roles abroad. Otherwise a sharp outreach message (`outreach-guide.md`) does more.

## Shape

- **Under 250 words**, 3-4 short paragraphs, one page. Use `cover_letters/template.tex`, or plain text for a form field.
- **Open with the match, not with yourself**: name the role, then the one piece of your work closest to their problem.
- **Middle paragraph: proof.** Two specifics from `profile/candidate.md` mapped to two of their must-haves. Numbers where the profile has them.
- **Why this company**, from real research: a product, an engineering blog post, a recent launch or funding round. Cite nothing you didn't actually read.
- **Close forward-looking**: what you'd want to work on in the first months, and availability (joining date or notice period).

## Avoid

"I am writing to apply for", "Respected Sir/Madam", "esteemed organisation", "kindly", "do the needful", "passionate", "hard-working and dedicated", and restating the CV line by line. Address the hiring manager by name if research finds one; otherwise "Dear Hiring Team".

Match the user's voice from `profile/voice.md`. If it has no samples, write plainly and directly.
