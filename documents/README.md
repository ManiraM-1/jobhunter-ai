# documents/

Drop your source material here before running `/setup`. Everything in this folder except this README is git-ignored.

Useful files:
- Your current CV (PDF or DOCX)
- LinkedIn profile export (on your profile: **More → Save to PDF**)
- Mark sheets or a degree certificate (for CGPA and percentages)
- Offer letters or internship certificates (for exact titles and dates)
- Past cover letters or LinkedIn posts you wrote (for your writing voice)
- Take-home assignment briefs, for `/takehome`
